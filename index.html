September 6–12, 2026

Seven entries. Four riding zones. One island.

This is the plan before the tires touch dirt: a week based in and around Cumberland, following the riding north to Campbell River, east to Hornby Island, and up toward Forbidden Plateau.

The route is still loose on purpose. Weather, legs and local advice will decide the open day.
